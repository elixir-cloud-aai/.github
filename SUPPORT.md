# Support

This guide applies to every repository of
[ELIXIR On-Cloud](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai); repositories don't
have their own `SUPPORT.md`.

## Where to go

- Questions and help with using our projects: the [Q&A][q-a] category of
  our [discussion board][discussions].
- Chat with the community: our [Slack][slack].
- Bugs and feature requests: the issue forms of the affected repository.
- Vulnerabilities: report them privately, as described in the
  [security policy](https://github.com/elixir-cloud-aai/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md),
  **never** in public issues or discussions.
- Anything else, including questions you can't ask in public: email
  [cloud-service@elixir-europe.org](mailto:cloud-service@elixir-europe.org).

## Before you ask

Read the documentation of the repository (linked from its README), and
search its issues and the existing discussions: your question may have been
answered already.

## What to include

- the repository and version (release or commit);
- what you want to do and what you tried;
- logs or error messages, without secrets or tokens.

## What to expect

Support is provided by the community on a best-effort basis, without
guaranteed response times. Support is in English.
